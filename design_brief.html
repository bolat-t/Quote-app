Main Screens
1
Home Screen (Hub)
Center tab · Tab 3
Main Tab
"This is the user's daily check-in with their virtual companion."
Core Action
Check in daily, earn XP, grow mascot
Primary Feeling
Cozy, motivating, alive
Retention Hook
Mascot evolves with user level
Primary UX Goal

Make the user feel rewarded for showing up. The mascot + XP bar should make progress feel tangible and delightful, not like a chore checklist. The whole screen should feel warm and alive — like greeting a pet or a friend.

What to Emphasize
The Mascot (Ulbo): This is the emotional heart of the screen. Large, charming, animated. The user taps it and it reacts (jump + haptic). It should feel like a living creature, not a static image. It's the visual centerpiece — everything else supports it.
XP Progress Bar: Sits below the mascot. Should feel satisfying to see fill up — not a dry stat bar but something that communicates "you're growing." Level title should be readable at a glance.
Quest Cards (5 daily tasks): These guide the user's day. Each card clearly shows the XP reward and whether it's done. Completed cards disappear — that disappearing should feel satisfying, animated out, not just hidden.
Greeting Text: Personalized time-of-day greeting. Should feel human, not boilerplate.
Evoke
Cozy Motivating Alive Morning ritual
2
Canvas Screen
Leftmost tab · Tab 1
Main Tab
"The user's daily creative reflection — a mini art journal paired with today's quote."
Core Action
Draw & reflect on today's quote
Primary Feeling
Creative, calm, personal
Retention Hook
Fresh quote + blank canvas each day
Primary UX Goal

Minimise friction between "I want to express something" and actually doing it. The interface should disappear and let the creative act take over. Every tap to draw should feel immediate and satisfying.

What to Emphasize
The Canvas Surface: The paper texture is what makes this feel like journaling, not just scribbling. The daily quote should be elegantly placed and readable — not like an afterthought on top of the drawing.
Drawing Controls (sidebar): Undo, color, clear — unobtrusive but always reachable. They must never visually compete with the canvas itself.
Drag-to-Delete Zone: When dragging a text/image box to the footer, a trash zone appears. Should be obvious and satisfying — clear visual feedback that the item will be deleted.
"Done" Flow: Saving is a meaningful moment. The overlay after tapping Done should feel celebratory — this is the user completing their daily creative act. Not a generic "saving…" spinner.
Evoke
Creative Calm Personal Quality journal
3
Journal Tab
Second tab · Tab 2
Main Tab
"A daily gratitude quest — users find positive things from their day."
Core Action
Fill 4 prompts, earn AI response
Primary Feeling
Playful, purposeful, rewarding
Retention Hook
Mascot reads and responds daily
Primary UX Goal

Make filling out all 4 entries feel easy and rewarding. The first prompt warms up the user with small things, then prompts deepen. It's structured like a journey, not homework — the UI should reinforce that sense of discovery.

What to Emphasize
The 4 Journal Entries: Each entry slot should feel distinct and inviting. As entries fill in, completed ones animate in (reveal effect). The visual progression from 0/4 → 4/4 should be immediately readable — a sense of momentum.
Status Indicators: Pending vs. completed items need a clear visual language. Completed entries should celebrate — checkmark animation, color shift, something that acknowledges the small win.
"Mark as Done" Button: The culmination of the exercise. Should feel like finishing a quest — not just submitting a form.
Spirit Response (AI mascot reply): After completing, Ulbo responds with personalized encouragement. This reveal should be a delightful moment — the mascot "reading" the entries and responding. Make it feel alive, not like a notification pop-up.
Evoke
Playful Purposeful Rewarding Mini-adventure
4
Journal / History Screen
Fourth tab · Tab 4
Main Tab
"A mood calendar and timeline — users look back at their emotional journey over time."
Core Action
Browse past entries by date
Primary Feeling
Reflective, insightful, nostalgic
Retention Hook
Mood history builds over time
Primary UX Goal

Make it effortless to navigate time and find meaning in patterns. The mood calendar is the primary navigation tool — it should be beautiful and immediately communicative. A glance at the month should show emotional ups and downs, like a personal heat map.

What to Emphasize
Mood Calendar (hero element): Each day cell shows a mood face (5 levels: rough → great). The month view should let the user instantly "feel" their emotional trends. Mood face icons must be expressive and distinct even at small sizes — this is more heat map than calendar.
Selected Day State: When tapping a date, content below updates to that day. The selected date must be unmistakably highlighted (yellow fill, black border) — it's the primary navigation act.
Timeline Entries: A chronological feed for the selected day — journal entries, hunt results, vision activity each need their own visual "card type." Scannable and distinct.
Activity Rings (top, optional): Entry count, streak, average mood at a glance. Visually striking but not overwhelming — secondary to the calendar.
Evoke
Reflective Insightful Nostalgic Seeing growth
5
Vision Board Screen
Fifth tab · Tab 5
Main Tab
"A personal inspiration collage — users curate images and affirmations that represent their goals and dreams."
Core Action
Build and arrange a vision collage
Primary Feeling
Aspirational, personal, beautiful
Retention Hook
Daily theme changes to spark new boards
Primary UX Goal

Let users build something that feels genuinely theirs. The board should look beautiful with minimal effort — layout presets and curated image collections do the heavy lifting, so users feel like a designer without needing to be one. A daily changing theme encourages returning to build something new each day.

What to Emphasize
The Board Canvas: Items should feel tactile — drag them, scale them (pinch), feel them settle into place. The board background theme sets the entire mood. This is the main surface — it should feel like a real canvas.
Image Browser: Browse by category → subcategory → thumbnail grid. Should feel like scrolling through inspiring Pinterest content. Fast loading, good placeholders, curated not random.
Text Affirmations: Style editor with font, color, background options. Text on a vision board must feel intentional and beautiful — not a plain label. Style options should be immediately visual (show previews, not just label names).
Layout Presets: One-tap arrangement (Grid, Scattered, Column, Mosaic). For users overwhelmed by manual placement — makes the board look polished instantly. Preset names should communicate the visual result clearly.
Export / Share: Sharing the vision board is a proud moment. The share flow should feel like presenting something you'd hang on your wall — not a standard OS share sheet.
Evoke
Aspirational Personal Beautiful Worth sharing
